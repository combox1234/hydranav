# Glossary

## Why it exists
Ensures standard terminology across the team.

## Who uses it
All engineers.

## When it is used
When writing documentation or naming variables.

## Terms
- **Navigable Water:** The area of water deemed safe for the boat to traverse.
- **Obstacle:** Any physical object (trash, buoy, dock) that must be avoided.
- **Costmap:** A 2D grid representing the cost of traversing specific areas.

## Current Status
Active.

## Future Responsibility
Expanded as new domain concepts emerge.
